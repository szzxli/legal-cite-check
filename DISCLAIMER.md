# Disclaimer

> **Not legal advice. This tool only checks whether a cited case exists in CourtListener and whether the case name matches. It does not check whether the case supports your argument, whether quotes or pin cites are accurate, or whether the case is still good law. A case marked NOT FOUND may still exist (CourtListener's coverage is incomplete); a case marked VERIFIED may still be wrong for your purpose. Read every case you cite.**

Longer explanation:

- CourtListener's coverage is incomplete, especially for state and
  unpublished cases. NOT FOUND means "not found in CourtListener", not
  "this case does not exist anywhere".
- VERIFIED means "a case exists at this citation and the name matches".
  It says nothing about whether the case supports your argument, whether
  any quote from it is accurate, or whether it is still good law. Those
  questions need a citator and a human reading the opinion.
- This tool never describes a user's citation as fabricated, fake, or
  hallucinated. Those are judgments about intent and provenance that an
  existence check cannot make. See `docs/labels.md`.
- If you are filing in court, read every case you cite, in full, from
  the official reporter or another authoritative source.
